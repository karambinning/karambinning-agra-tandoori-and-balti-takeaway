1980 — the year we opened

Same kitchen. Same street. More or less the same menu.

Shabir opened the doors of Agra Tandoori in 1980, on Liverpool Road, in the middle of Fenton — one of Stoke-on-Trent's six pottery towns. Forty-six years of tandoor smoke up the chimney, and a queue that turns over at five sharp.

Customers tell us they've been ordering the chicken spinach balti since their kids were small. Now their kids order it too. Some things you don't change.

Tuesdays are quiet — the kitchen rests, the cold store gets restocked, Shabir reads the paper. Every other night, from five until quarter to midnight, the lights are on.
